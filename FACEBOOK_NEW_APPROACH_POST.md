# Facebook Post: New Telegram Deal Format

## Main Version

Calgary grocery deal update:

I am changing how the weekly deal alerts are posted so they are easier to scan and more useful while you are actually planning a shop.

Instead of one long mixed list, the Telegram channel will now send 3 focused messages:

1. Proteins
Whole raw base proteins come first, like chicken breast, whole chicken, roasts, ground meat, pork tenderloin, fish, and seafood. Prepared items still show up, but they come later, so breaded/stuffed/marinated options do not crowd out the basic meal-building deals.

2. Vegetables
This is focused on vegetable produce, like lettuce, tomatoes, corn, cucumbers, potatoes, carrots, celery, peppers, and similar items.

3. Pantry and other grocery deals
This covers the rest of the useful grocery staples, like pasta sauce, dairy, frozen items, snacks, beverages, bakery, and other food deals.

The big upgrade: deal names are clickable. When you tap one, it opens a simple snapshot card showing the price history, current price, historical average, high/low range, score, confidence, and a small chart so you can quickly see whether it is actually a good deal.

The goal is not just to find cheap items. The goal is to make the list practical:

- Proteins first for meal planning
- Vegetables separated so they do not get buried
- Pantry and extras in their own message
- Prepared proteins lower in the protein list
- Price-history snapshots attached to the deals

This should make the weekly updates easier to skim, easier to trust, and easier to turn into an actual grocery plan.

Telegram channel:
https://t.me/CalgaryGroceryDeals

Feedback welcome. If a category feels too broad, too narrow, or missing something useful, I can keep tuning it.
